Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Who we are

Baby Dino Pals is an independent channel producing animated videos for young children. This page describes how our publishing tools handle data.

What we access

We operate a private tool that uploads our own videos to our own channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. To do this the tool uses official APIs and requests access strictly limited to:

  • uploading videos to the Baby Dino Pals YouTube channel;
  • publishing posts to the Baby Dino Pals Instagram account;
  • reading basic account information (account name and ID) to confirm the correct destination.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers.
  • We do not access other people's accounts, contacts, messages or private content.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use tracking, cookies or analytics on this website.

Access tokens

Authentication tokens are stored locally on a single personal computer, with file permissions restricted to the account owner. They are never transmitted anywhere except to the official APIs of the platforms they belong to, and can be revoked at any time by the account owner from the platform's own settings.

Children's privacy

Our videos are made for children, and all our uploads are marked as "Made for Kids" where the platform provides that setting. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Viewer data is handled entirely by the platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) under their own privacy policies.

AI disclosure

Our videos are created with the help of AI tools. Every upload is labelled accordingly on each platform.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to babydinopals@gmail.com.
